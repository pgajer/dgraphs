## Analysis and data contract

Six original-expression candidates were evaluated under a frozen coverage rule. Overlapping 256- and 300-profile PreSSMat subsets were selected for longer adaptive behavior, then compared with evaluated Python and native implementations.

## Observed results

The selected inputs perform 182 and 176 pruning iterations, including retuning and isolate creation. Native/evaluated comparisons pass. Original-expression controls make the same discrete decisions but exceed intermediate-array limits on both inputs.

This record summarizes two overlapping long-history examples. The frozen report and evidence manifest retain the detailed tables/traces; no additional standalone plot is needed for this question.

## Interpretation and limitations

Selection favors coverage, not representative performance. The subsets overlap and are not independent biological replication. Equal final decisions do not erase failed intermediate-array comparisons.
