# Descriptive question

Result headline with execution state and evidence provenance. Current historical
review and report-review status are injected from audit-summary.json.

## Question and motivation

State alternatives and why the comparison matters; distinguish prospective from
retrospective motivation.

## Methods and data contract

Identify input unit, transformations, comparators, numerical policy, selection,
reuse, identities and what was actually varied.

## Results and figure

Define denominators and units. A failed or unexecuted outcome is explicit. State
why a figure is absent if no appropriate evidence asset exists.

## Interpretation and limits

Separate observed agreement, proposed mechanism and untested use.

## Verification and next step

Describe author checks, historical independent scope and current report review.
Link the issue register and typed successor relationships.

## Sources and reproduction

Link manifest, original code/plan/ledger and any figure-reconstruction command.
Do not infer execution from a new source hash or run solvers during rendering.
