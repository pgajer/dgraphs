tenenbaum2000
verified		 Introduction: Isomap estimates intrinsic geodesic distances using shortest paths in a neighborhood graph before applying classical multidimensional scaling. The paper defines Isomap by estimating geodesic distances as shortest paths in a neighborhood graph and applying classical MDS to the resulting distance matrix. source Tenenbaum, de Silva, and Langford (2000), Science 290(5500), 2319--2323, DOI 10.1126/science.290.5500.2319.
lin1991
verified		 Introduction: Jensen--Shannon divergence is an entropy-based dissimilarity for probability distributions and can therefore be applied to relative-abundance compositions. Section I derives Jensen--Shannon divergence from Jensen's inequality and Shannon entropy, describes it as a measure of differences among probability distributions, and notes its applicability to biological taxonomy. source; accessible article PDF Lin (1991), IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 37(1), 145--151, DOI 10.1109/18.61115. Verified against the complete article and DOI metadata on 2026-09-01.
endres2003
verified		 Introduction: the square root of Jensen--Shannon divergence, up to an immaterial positive constant factor, is a metric on probability distributions. Section II identifies the squared quantity as Jensen--Shannon divergence up to convention, and Theorem 1 proves that its square root satisfies the metric axioms. source; author-institution repository Endres and Schindelin (2003), IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 49(7), 1858--1860, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2003.813506. Verified against the complete article and DOI metadata on 2026-09-01.
