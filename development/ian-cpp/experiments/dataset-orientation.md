# Dataset and fixture orientation

| Family | What it represents | Reuse and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Historical combined Hellinger subproblems | Saved LPs from 4,849 specimens represented by 4,841 unique profiles | Fixed-problem replay, not a new combined-cohort fit or biological comparison. |
| PreSSMat pilot and subsets | An authorized 500-profile source and deterministic smaller/perturbed subsets | Overlapping tests; not independent biological replication. No outcomes used for fixture selection. |
| Small synthetic geometries | Curves, patches, nearby arms, helix and saddle | Engineering coverage; held-out geometry does not automatically supply a scientific oracle. |
| 500-profile scale panel | Density-varying helix, noisy cloud, separated disks and PreSSMat | Different adaptive histories and graph components; policies differ across milestones. |
| 1,000-profile frozen panel | Helix, cloud, lobes and quadratic surfaces | Only the helix has paired attempts in the scale milestone; six later inputs are gated. |
| dgraphs quadforms | Intrinsic dimensions 2-5, ambient dimensions 3-6; latent-box uniform sampling | Geometry construction verified, IAN trajectories unexecuted; not uniform surface-area samples. |
| Fixed terminal LP collection | Difficult saved states, including two nearly equal helix problems | Exact identities matter: one RHS binary64 step can change solver behavior. |
| Future scientific benchmarks | Known geometry and prescribed smooth conditional means under a sampling/noise model | Proposed only; no scientific recovery result exists yet. |

The fixture register links original manifests. Cohort specimen IDs and raw molecular
measurements are not copied into this catalogue. Profile equality does not establish
participant equality. Further real-data work requires a separate scientific contract.
